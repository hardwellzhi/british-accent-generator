# British Accent Generator

AI British / UK accent text-to-speech with **315** regionally labelled voices — RP and standard British, young British, Scottish, Welsh, and Northern Irish.

**Live product:** [https://britishaccent.pro/](https://britishaccent.pro/)

## What it does

- Preview speakers, paste a script, generate and download natural UK English audio
- Built for videos, lessons, podcasts, demos, and training
- Also: Voice Clone, Voice Design, Voice Effects, and AI Lip Sync
- Freemium: free daily credits; credit packs and subscriptions for longer scripts and premium features
